# Observing sites for the harnesses to borrow, when the phone's own position is
# the wrong one to measure from.
#
# doze-check.sh needs the app to be polling on a schedule it can see the shape
# of. PollingPolicy decides that from the sky, not from the interval in the
# settings: it sleeps until dark while the sun is up, and backs off to
# OVERCAST_MINUTES under heavy cloud. Neither leaves a gap worth measuring, and
# on a phone in Britain at ten in the morning there is nothing to be done about
# it. So the harness borrows somewhere that is dark and clear right now.
#
# What makes a site good here:
#
#   - Real darkness every night of the year. Everything below is inside 44
#     degrees of the equator, so the sun clears civil twilight at midnight even
#     at the solstice. A higher site would strand the harness in DORMANT or
#     Darkness.None for weeks at a time.
#   - Spread in longitude, so that whatever the hour in Britain, several of
#     these are in the middle of their night. The widest gap below is 45
#     degrees, three hours, against a night that lasts eight or more.
#   - Somewhere an observer would actually stand, so a run that does provoke a
#     notification reads like a real one.
#
# Latitude, longitude and name, whitespace separated, name last and may contain
# spaces. Longitudes are -180 to 180, the way the app takes them, not the 0-359
# the aurora grid is indexed by.
